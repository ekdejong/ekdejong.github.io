## Research

I study cloud microphysics -- the aerosols and droplets that make up clouds -- and how we represent them in climate models, working at the intersection of machine learning, high-fidelity simulation, and observational inference. My work includes:

- ML emulators and reduced-order models for how particle size distributions evolve
- Probabilistic inference of 3D cloud properties from satellite observations
- High-fidelity simulation using the Superdroplet Method and E3SM/EAMxx
- Seasonal predictability of coupled AI weather/climate emulators

The throughline connects micron-scale cloud physics to global climate predictions, especially around aerosol-cloud interactions -- one of the largest remaining sources of uncertainty in climate projections.
