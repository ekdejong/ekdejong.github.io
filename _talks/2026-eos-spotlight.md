Our paper on data-driven reduced-order modeling for warm rain microphysics was featured as an *Eos* Research Spotlight. Science writer Nathaniel Scharping covers how we compared three machine-learning approaches to modeling droplet coalescence, and why the simplest one (a SINDy-based framework) won out. [Read the spotlight here](https://eos.org/research-spotlights/comparing-machine-learning-models-of-raindrop-formation), or check out [the full paper](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JH001103).
